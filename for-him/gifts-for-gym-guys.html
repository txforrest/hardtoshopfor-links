Quick buying tips

  • Check what gym he goes to. Some ban loose chalk or certain gear.
  • For belts and clothing, look at what size he already wears, or pick adjustable items.
  • If he's a beginner, recovery gear (foam roller, massage gun) is safer than technical lifting gear.
