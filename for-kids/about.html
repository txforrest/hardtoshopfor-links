About This Site

Shopping for kids is harder than it looks. They want everything in the commercial, and most of it is forgotten by New Year's. This site helps you pick toys they'll keep playing with.

It's part of the Hard-to-Shop-For family with The Hard-to-Shop-For Guy and The Hard-to-Shop-For Gal, built in Texas. Every pick is chosen for real play value and the right age, not a one-day wow.

We don't sell anything directly. Links send you to Amazon, where you can compare ratings and prices and check delivery dates before you buy.
